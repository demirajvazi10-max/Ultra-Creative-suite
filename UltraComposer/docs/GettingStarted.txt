ULTRA COMPOSER - GETTING STARTED
=================================

Thanks for installing Ultra Composer, part of the Ultra Creative Suite - an
accessible, keyboard-first digital audio workstation, built from day one to
work equally well for blind and sighted musicians.

FIRST STEPS

1. Launch Ultra Composer. It opens on the "Instrument Bank" view and starts
   its audio engine automatically.
2. Press Tab until focus reaches "Performance keyboard".
3. Play a few notes: Z S X D C V G B H N J M for one octave, Q 2 W 3 E R 5 T
   6 Y 7 U for the octave above. Page Up/Page Down shift the octave.
4. A default instrument sound (SoundFont bank) is already bundled in and
   ready to go - no setup needed to hear something right away.
5. Have a MIDI keyboard? Pick it from the "MIDI device" list, click
   "Refresh devices" if needed, then "Connect".

WHERE TO GO NEXT

Ultra Composer has five main views, reachable from the "View" menu:
Instrument Bank, Sequencer, Arranger, Multi-track & Mixer, and
Auto-accompaniment. Every one of them is fully operable and announced
through a screen reader (JAWS was used throughout development).

For detailed, step-by-step instructions on every feature - what each
button does, how the Sequencer's note list works, how Auto-accompaniment
follows your chords, how to save a project, and more - open the built-in
User Guide from inside the app:

    Help menu -> User Guide

That guide is also fully bilingual (English/Serbian, switchable from the
Language menu) and stays up to date with the app itself, so it's the best
place to look things up as you go.

ULTRA COMPOSER - POČETNO UPUTSTVO
==================================

Hvala što si instalirao/la Ultra Composer, deo Ultra Creative Suite-a -
pristupačnu, tastaturi prilagođenu digitalnu audio radnu stanicu, napravljenu
od samog početka da podjednako dobro radi i za slepe i za videće muzičare.

PRVI KORACI

1. Pokreni Ultra Composer. Program se otvara na prikazu "Banka instrumenata"
   i sam pokreće audio motor.
2. Pritiskaj Tab dok fokus ne stigne do "Klavijatura za sviranje".
3. Odsviraj nekoliko nota: Z S X D C V G B H N J M za jednu oktavu, Q 2 W 3
   E R 5 T 6 Y 7 U za oktavu iznad. Page Up/Page Down pomeraju oktavu.
4. Podrazumevani zvuk instrumenta (SoundFont banka) je već ugrađen i
   spreman - nije potrebno nikakvo podešavanje da bi odmah nešto čuo/la.
5. Imaš MIDI klavijaturu? Izaberi je iz liste "MIDI uređaj", klikni
   "Osveži uređaje" ako je potrebno, pa "Poveži".

GDE DALJE

Ultra Composer ima pet glavnih prikaza, dostupnih iz menija "Prikaz":
Banka instrumenata, Sekvenser, Aranžer, Multi-trake i mikser, i
Auto-pratnja. Svaki od njih je potpuno funkcionalan i najavljuje se kroz
čitač ekrana (razvoj je vršen uz JAWS).

Za detaljno, korak-po-korak uputstvo za svaku funkciju - šta koje dugme
radi, kako radi spisak nota u Sekvenseru, kako Auto-pratnja prati akorde
koje sviraš, kako se čuva projekat, i još mnogo toga - otvori ugrađeno
Uputstvo za upotrebu direktno iz programa:

    Meni "Pomoć" -> "Uputstvo za upotrebu"

To uputstvo je takođe potpuno dvojezično (srpski/engleski, menja se iz
menija "Jezik") i prati sam program, pa je najbolje mesto da tražiš
odgovore dok radiš.
